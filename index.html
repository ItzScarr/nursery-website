A bright start for every child

Care built around your child.

Brightstart Nurseries began in 2012 with a simple aim: give children a safe, stimulating place to grow and families the confidence to feel at home.

We encourage independence and self-assurance through play, exploration and supportive relationships. Each child has a dedicated key person who learns about their interests, follows their development and works closely with their family.

Nursery Funding

Families in England may be able to use funded childcare hours or other support towards the cost of a nursery place. What you can claim depends on your child's age and circumstances.

Ask your chosen nursery how funding may fit with the sessions you need, and about availability, meals, additional hours and any other charges.

Ask us about funding

Working parents' funded hours

Eligible working parents may receive up to 30 hours of funded childcare a week for 38 weeks of the year, from the term after their child turns 9 months until school age.

Support for 2-year-olds

Some 2-year-olds qualify for 15 hours of funded early learning when their family or the child receives additional support.

All 3- and 4-year-olds

Every 3- and 4-year-old in England can access 15 funded hours a week for 38 weeks of the year.

Tax-Free Childcare

Eligible families may also be able to use Tax-Free Childcare towards approved childcare costs.

Check your eligibility and application dates with the GOV.UK childcare support checker. Discuss funded hours and any extra costs with the nursery before booking.
