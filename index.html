A bright start for every child

Care built around your child.

Brightstart Nurseries began in 2012 with a simple aim: give children a safe, stimulating place to grow and families the confidence to feel at home.

We encourage independence and self-assurance through play, exploration and supportive relationships. Each child has a dedicated key person who learns about their interests, follows their development and works closely with their family.

How we care

Every child, every step of the way.

Familiar routines, close relationships and room to explore help children feel secure and curious.

🤝

A person who knows your child

A dedicated key person helps each child settle, plans around their interests and shares milestones with families. A buddy provides continuity of care.

🌿

Play inside and out

Child-led play, creative activities and outdoor experiences support learning, confidence, physical development and friendships.

🥕

Food made on site

On-site kitchens prepare balanced meals and snacks. The team caters for allergies, intolerances, sensory preferences and religious requirements, and supports babies through weaning.

Nursery life

Room for routine. Room for discovery.

Days are structured from arrival to home time, with flexibility to follow children's changing needs, interests and individual sleep routines.

Welcome and breakfastArrival, self-registration and child-led play.
Explore and learnCircle time, music, guided activities, stories and outdoor play.
Meals and restSnacks, lunch, sleep or quiet time according to each child.
Afternoon adventuresMore play, movement and learning before tea and home time.
Learning meets play and every moment counts.

Every child's day combines familiar routines with time to explore.
